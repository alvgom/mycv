I am writing to express my interest in the advertised position at Centaura. I am a PhD student due to graduate this year, and I trust that my profile and interests fit well with the information I could find from your startup. 
My field of expertise is Computer Vision applied to biomedical data. During my career I have been confident that computational methods are now key to further advance in biology, and have thus been involved in projects towards this end. 

Both during my PhD thesis and as project manager, I have worked with data scientists as well as biomedical researchers. Such collaboration has been possible thanks to my education on both fields. After my studies in Biomedical Engineering, I started gathering experience in the web lab, especially on sample preparation for wide tissue fluorescence microscopy. I then decided resolved that the major bottleneck in biomedical research was not in data generation, but rather in its thorough analysis. Since then, I have been researching on Machine Learning methods to facilitate data analysis in biomedical datasets.

Although from the job description I understand that datasets in Centaura may not consists of images, I have experienced that with the recent advances in artificial intelligence, namely with deep neural networks, that acquired skills are easily transferable among diverse datasets. Consequently, I believe my experience both in academy and industry makes me an ideal candidate for this position. 

I am currently looking for challenging and inspiring projects where I can apply my research experience to potentially have a positive impact in the world. Reading about your objective of fighting aging was inspiring and connected perfectly with the reasons why I decided to devote my professional life to the field where I have gathered my expertise. Thank you for your consideration.

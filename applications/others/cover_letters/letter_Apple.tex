\begin{flushleft}
While my PhD in Computer Vision at ETH Zurich established my expertise in deep learning and novel AI methodologies, it is my 5+ years in the pharmaceutical industry at Roche that taught me how to translate this research into tangible healthcare impact. I have spent the last years transitioning complex machine learning methodologies into scalable clinical products.  

Deploying models in this space is inherently challenging. It requires navigating exceptionally high standards for clinical validation, operating within highly regulated environments, and building scientific credibility with diverse, cross-disciplinary stakeholders. My recent work has centered heavily on this exact translation: designing rigorous evaluation protocols, assessing model robustness beyond standard metrics, and performing the necessary analyses to ensure clinical reliability.  

I am excited about the PAIS team because it aligns perfectly with my background. Beyond my hands-on technical expertise in foundation models, multi-modality, and statistical evaluation, I bring the unique perspective of building and defending AI products in the pharmaceutical industry. I learned what it takes to execute regulatory-grade validation, rigorously interrogate model behavior, and build the cross-functional trust required to deploy clinical products at scale.  

I would welcome the opportunity to bring this perspective to Apple's health applications. Thank you for your time and consideration.

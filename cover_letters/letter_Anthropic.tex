\begin{flushleft}
I am excited to apply for this position at Anthropic. With a PhD in Computer Vision from ETH Zurich and over four additional years of experience developing AI products in the pharmaceutical industry, I bring a combination of technical expertise, collaborative leadership, and a passion for creating transformative AI solutions. 

In my current role at Roche, a global leader in the pharmaceutical industry, I have led the development of innovative products that leverage advanced machine learning to create unique solutions while ensuring rigorous safety and evaluation standards. Highlights include leading the creation of a company-wide vision foundation model for digital pathology, designing an AI assistant that accelerates clinical trials by analyzing colonoscopy videos, and creating a novel unsupervised domain adaptation model in ophthalmology to extend the use of existing products to new medical devices. I have also helped drive more effective outcomes for our projects by implementing cross-functional matrix teams and integrating best practices in software engineering in our group.

I am personally driven by working on projects that bring a meaningful societal impact. While my recent focus has been on improving the lives of patients through AI-driven healthcare solutions, I am increasingly inspired by the potential of AI assistants, such as those Anthropic is pioneering. I believe these assistants have the potential to not only accelerate projects like those I have worked on, i.e. using AI for healthcare applications, but also enable countless other impactful applications, such as improving education accessibility or environmental conservation. This is why I am inspired by Anthropic’s mission of building safe, ethical and groundbreaking AI systems. Moreover, while my expertise has centered on computer vision, the recent advancements in AI have made it clear to me that the future lies in models that seamlessly integrate multiple modalities. Therefore I am eager to be part of these advances at Anthropic.

Anthropic’s commitment to responsibility and collaboration deeply resonates with me, as these are values I have kept throughout my career. I am confident that my experience in cutting-edge AI research, coupled with my ability to deliver impactful, user-focused products, positions me to make a meaningful contribution to your vision. I would be delighted to bring my skills and enthusiasm to help advance Anthropic's groundbreaking work in AI.

\end{flushleft}

\begin{flushleft}

I recently learned about the opening of the Department of Quantitative Biomedicine at UZH and was excited about the new research possibilities in Zurich related to my field. The focus of your group on machine learning applications in biomedicine is of particular interest to me, and it is thus with great enthusiasm that I have read about the open postdoc position in your group, and I would hereby like to formally express my interest in this position.

I have always been passionate about addressing biomedical research problems from a computational perspective. After studying a Bachelor’s and a Master’s  degree in biomedical engineering at the Universidad Carlos III in Madrid and at ETH Zurich, respectively – for both of which I was awarded a prize for graduating top of my class – I chose to undertake a PhD in the field of computer vision for biomedical images. My thesis, which I am due to complete within the next months, has been a close collaboration between the Computer Vision Lab at ETH Zurich and the University Hospital Zurich, and has focused on gaining insight into blood cell production processes through image-based characterization of state-of-the-art datasets. My latest research activity has consisted in facilitating the application of deep neural networks for segmentation in complex 3D fluorescence microscopy datasets, with a focus on attention mechanisms, and more recently on semi-supervised and contrastive learning.

In the course of these projects I have acquired a solid understanding of the mathematical foundations of artificial intelligence, and mastered machine learning frameworks such as Tensorflow. The results of my research have been published in renowned scientific journals, including in Nature Communications, and one manuscript is currently under revision in Nature Machine Intelligence. Moreover, I have contributed to research collaborations the results of which have been published in a range of journals in different fields, including Cell Stem Cell. Finally, I have presented my findings at a variety of scientific conferences including at ISBI, and I received the “best presentation award” at the Day of Clinical Research at University Hospital of Zurich 2018.

Besides my research experience, I have remotely managed a team of engineers in the company MicroscopeIT as part of a bioimage informatics project. In both the industrial and academic sides of my career, I have worked with multidisciplinary teams, which has allowed me to develop better leadership and communication skills. In addition, integrating information from different fields influenced me towards a more independent approach of conceiving and organizing my own ideas.

I am currently looking for new challenges towards which I can direct my research activity, while also expanding my career horizon towards more clinically oriented applications and studying different machine learning techniques beyond vision, namely for natural language processing or Omics data. I am therefore convinced that a postdoc in your group is the perfect opportunity for me to grow professionally, and I trust that my experience matches the required qualifications.

Thank you for taking the time to review my application. I am highly motivated to apply my machine learning expertise to the different challenges in clinical datasets, and I would be happy to present myself personally. 



\end{flushleft}
